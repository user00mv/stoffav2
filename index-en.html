About the company

About Us

Stoffa s.r.o. was founded with a single vision: to manufacture sample books that serve not just as practical tools, but as true statements of quality. In a market that prioritizes speed, we choose precision.

Our sample books are produced in the Czech Republic, combining modern production technology with an uncompromising attention to detail. Every piece that leaves our facility is the result of expertise and deep respect for material and craft.

We work with designers, fabric manufacturers, fashion brands, and distributors — everyone who understands that presentation is as important as the product itself.
